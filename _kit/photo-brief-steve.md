# Photo brief — Lindsey Channel paintings (for Steve)

These photos are the whole sale online. A buyer judges a $2,000 painting from a phone screen.

**For every painting:**
1. **Straight-on, full painting.** Camera square to the canvas, even light from both sides at ~45° (no flash, no window glare on the varnish), color as true as you can get (gray card or a white-balance shot helps). Crop right to the canvas edge. Long side **2000px or more**, JPG.
2. **1–2 detail shots.** Brushwork close-ups. Oils sell on texture.

**For originals that are for sale, also:**
3. **One "on a wall" shot** for scale: hung in a room, or leaning beside something people know the size of.

**Plus:**
4. **Lindsey**: a portrait, ideally at the easel or in the studio. Used on the About section.

**Naming:** name each file with the painting's title so we can match it to the catalog, e.g. `Glacier Morning.jpg`, `Glacier Morning detail 1.jpg`. We'll rename on our end.

**Sold paintings:** if there are older photos of sold work, send those too, even imperfect. A sold painting with a decent photo still earns trust.
