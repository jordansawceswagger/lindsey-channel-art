# 20-minute conversation with Lindsey (record it)

The about section, the hero line and every painting description should come from his words, not ours. Record this (phone voice memo is fine), get it transcribed, write from the transcript, and he approves every line before it goes up.

**Start**
- When did you start painting? What got you going in 2005 (or whenever it really started)?
- Who taught you, or who did you learn from by looking?

**The subjects**
- Why these subjects? Where did the connection come from?
- Are there specific people, places or tribes in the paintings? How do you want that described, and what should we never say?
- Is there anything about how the work is described that's bothered you before?

**The work**
- Walk me through how one painting gets made, start to finish.
- How long does a big one take?
- Which painting are you proudest of and why?
- Which sold one do you miss?

**Selling**
- Where has your work sold before (galleries, shows, private)? Any collectors or places we can name?
- Do you take commissions? What's the process and a typical price range?
- Prints: which paintings are you OK making prints of? Limited editions or open?

**One line**
- If someone asked "what do you paint?" at a gas station, what would you say?
